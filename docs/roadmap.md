# Research roadmap

Progression is gated by discriminating evidence, not by visual complexity or additional features. Every stage remains a synthetic software experiment unless separately validated against an appropriate empirical system.

| Stage | Objective | Capability | Success gate | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V0 — Proof of concept** | Verify that matched synthetic assays distinguish controlled mechanisms. | Deterministic subjects, fixed interventions, snapshots, forks, and receipts. | Recover the known synthetic distinctions on held-out seeds after the learning preflight passes. | Revise the task or mechanisms; do not make an active-discovery claim. |
| **V1 — Experimental system** | Test adaptive hypothesis updates against fixed and random panels. | Posterior predictions and information-gain selection over a frozen grammar. | Improve held-out prediction under equal assay budgets with acceptable calibration. | Expand held-out families and audit misspecification and calibration. |
| **V2 — Research platform** | Test whether grammar revision adds value beyond active selection. | Revisable typed assay grammar, named ablations, null controls, preregistration, and raw receipts. | Beat the frozen-grammar active selector on the locked held-out criteria under equal probe and compute budgets. | Publish the null or inconclusive result and retain the simpler method. |
| **V3 — Frontier experiment** | Test transfer across broader synthetic lineages and developmental rules. | Plastic development, varied tissue layouts, and cross-lineage transfer assays. | Replicated prediction on unseen synthetic lineages and contexts. | Map the boundary conditions and narrow the claim. |

The current implementation is a V0 software workbench with a V1/V2 compiler comparison scaffold. Its smoke run is a workflow check, not evidence that a stage's scientific gate has passed. Biological validation is outside this roadmap's current implementation and would require a separate research design, domain expertise, and evidence.
